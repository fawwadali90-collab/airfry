Air Fryer Buying Guide: How to Choose

Quick answer: For 1–2 people, buy a 4–5 qt single basket. For 3+ people, buy a dual basket so two foods finish together. Check basket shape, wattage, cleaning, and warranty before you check the price.

FAQ

Q: Single or dual basket?
A: Dual if you cook a main + side together. Single if you cook one batch and want a smaller, cheaper fryer.

Q: What size do I really need?
A: Measure your counter and your usual batch — litres/quarts on the box include both baskets on dual models.
