Crispy Air Fryer Chicken

Quick answer: Air fry chicken at 400°F / 200°C for 18–22 minutes, flipping halfway, until it reaches 165°F / 74°C inside. Dry the skin, use a single layer, and rest 5 minutes for crispy, juicy chicken.

The recipe that builds trust: simple, tested, and it works the first time.

Temp: 400°F (200°C)   Time: 18–22 minutes, flip halfway   Serves: 4

You need: chicken thighs or breasts, oil spray, salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder.

Steps: 1. Pat chicken dry — dry skin = crispy skin. 2. Spray lightly with oil, season. 3. Single layer, don't crowd the basket. 4. Air fry at 400°F / 200°C, flip halfway, cook until internal temp is 165°F / 74°C. 5. Rest 5 minutes before cutting.

The fryer used here: a 5–6 qt basket fryer is ideal for 4 servings.

Why it goes crispy (and why yours didn't)

Wet chicken steams. Crowded baskets steam. A single layer, dry surface, and high heat fix both.
