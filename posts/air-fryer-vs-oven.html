Air Fryer vs Oven: When It Actually Saves Money

Short answer: for small meals, the air fryer usually wins — it preheats in minutes and heats a small space. For a big family roast or a full tray bake, the oven still wins.

Time

Air fryer: 2–3 minute preheat. Oven: 10–15 minutes. That alone saves time on weeknights.

Cost

Cost depends on your electricity price and how long you cook. Don't quote a single "saves 70%" number — show the maths: watts × hours × your price per kWh. Readers trust that more.

Taste

Air fryer wins for crispiness on small batches. Oven wins for even baking and large batches.
